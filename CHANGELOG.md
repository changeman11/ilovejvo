# I<3JVO changelog

Player-facing changes, newest first. Each entry is what was posted (or ready to post) in Discord #i-love-jvowebapp.
When writing the next one: `git log --since="<date of the entry below>"`.

---

## 2026-10-25 — the big one (everything since 22 Oct)

### 🎮 Match nights now run themselves
- Matches open at the start time, one at a time per team: your next match opens as soon as your last score is in
- ✋ **Ready up**: go to your match page and press I'm ready. The match starts when 4 from each team are ready (new events)
- Report the score on the match page **or in the match's Discord thread**. When both teams agree, it goes live instantly
- 👑 Captains can confirm the other team's score with one tap
- ☕ A 15-minute break before the finals, with a countdown, then the finals open by themselves
- Stuck match (scores don't match, or nobody reports)? Admins get pinged after 10 minutes
- 📜 Every event page now shows its **Event rules**

### 🧩 Groups & finals
- Group stages: automatic or hand-picked groups, a table per group, then a top 2/4/8 knockout (group mates don't meet in the first round)
- A big finals bracket on every event page
- 🥉 Optional 3rd place match
- Fixtures page: one section per event, plus a new 🏆 Groups & bracket view with a seeding map
- Home and away are now shared out fairly (no more whole group stage away)

### 📊 Stats
- Stats are read automatically from your end-of-match screenshot. Nobody types numbers
- In your match thread: **📊 Check your stats**. Confirm your line or fix it; captains can confirm the whole team
- ❓ **Who's who?** If your in-game name didn't match, pick yourself from the drop-down (the screenshot is shown big)
- Post the screenshot in the match thread and the bot grabs it (it ignores GIFs and memes)
- Redesigned Stats page: award cards for each stat's leaders, 🥇🥈🥉, clearer names
- Results show whether a match's stats are confirmed

### 🏆 Awards & trophies
- Discord roles for the **top 3 in every stat**, and they move as the leaderboards change: 👑 Point Machine, 💥 Spike Royalty, 🧱 The Wall, 🛡️ Receive Master, 🪄 Set Wizard, 🤝 Playmaker, 🎯 Ace Sniper, 😈 Dig Demon
- 🏆 Champions announcement on the site. Congrats **Chikchester City**! (🥈 Sets on the Beach, 🥉 sensayy=squeaker fc)
- Trophy cabinet on club pages, and 🏆🥈🥉 next to club names on the Teams tab

### 🤖 Pickup games & 1v1 (Discord)
- Pickup: sign up from the menu in #pickup or on the site's Pickup page
- A random 2v2–6v6 mode each game, or vote for the next mode
- Locked ready-up channel, two random captains draft the teams, and the bot moves you into your team's voice channel
- Elo ratings and leaderboards (/elo, /leaderboard, and on the site)
- ⚔️ 1v1 ranked queue with RP in #1v1-queue
- Every event match gets its own Discord thread, with both squads pinged
- Your club gets its own coloured Discord role, and managers get the Captain role
- New members get a welcome pointing them to the site

### 🛡️ Clubs
- Squads are 4 + 2 subs (6 max)
- Leave your club straight away (no admin needed). Moves pause while an event is on
- Pick free agents to invite while you register a club; invites go out when it's approved
- 🙋 Solo sign-ups: no club? Sign up solo and admins group you into a team
- LFT tag for free agents looking for a team
- 📰 Transfer news in the bottom ticker
- Clubs disband by themselves when their last player leaves
- The Teams tab lists every club's squad

### 👤 Profiles
- Picture editor: drag, zoom and centre your pictures. GIFs keep moving, and big GIFs (up to 30 MB) shrink automatically
- Staff badges next to names: 🛡️ Admin, 🔨 Mod, 🎙️ Caster

### 🏠 Home & site
- ⭐ FEATURED! event card with a countdown in your own time zone; it becomes a live board during events
- 🔴 Live now panel: matches on and the latest scores
- The whole site updates live, so no more refreshing
- 15 more community characters (34 in total), and they idle-sway now
- **Post your character in #in-game-characters and the bot adds it to the site by itself ✅**
- A "site updated" bar appears when there's a new version
